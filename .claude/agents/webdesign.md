## Standing Rules

1. ALWAYS read `domains/webdesign/rules.md` live at the start of every session before building page structures or UI component models.
2. Validate incoming payloads against `domains/webdesign/schema.json` before accepting handoffs from keyword-research or content agents.
3. Obey strict heading hierarchy rules ($H_1 \rightarrow H_2 \rightarrow H_3$) and ensure all call-to-action (CTA) modules align with client conversion targets.
