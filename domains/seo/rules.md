# SEO Domain Rule Pack

## Scope & Governance
Governs all local search optimization, citation consistency, schema markup rendering, and business post generation.

## Mandatory Brand Guardrails
- **Zero Em-Dashes:** Em-dashes (`—`) are strictly prohibited in all local post body text. Use commas or standard hyphens instead.
- **NAP & CTA Field Isolation:** Phone numbers and raw website URLs MUST NOT appear inside the body paragraph. They belong exclusively in dedicated CTA button payloads or structured location schema objects.
- **Review Count Threshold:** Never surface client review counts unless the verified total exceeds 100+.
